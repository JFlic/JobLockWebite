The apps stay locked until you've applied.

JobLock shields your worst distractions while you send job applications, and unshields them when you've done the work.

How it works

  1. Pick the apps that pull you away from applying.
  2. Start a session. They go behind a shield.
  3. Apply to a job and screenshot the confirmation. JobLock reads it, logs it, and clears the screenshot off your camera roll.
  4. Hit your number and the shields drop.

Why bother

Over a career, people who change employers out-earn people who stay — ADP puts median pay growth at 6.5% a year for job-changers against 4.4% for job-stayers. The hard part was never knowing that. It is sitting down and sending the applications.

Your job log stays on your phone

No account, no server, no ads. Your job log lives on your iPhone and nowhere else, and confirmation screenshots are read once and then discarded rather than stored.
